01 / The program

You know how to take initiative. Now build on it.

Service develops the ability to make decisions, adapt quickly, solve unfamiliar problems, and take responsibility for a team. Cherry Seed brings those capabilities into entrepreneurship through structured learning, practical work, and experienced guidance.

  • AAcademic knowledge
  • BPractical startup tools
  • CMentorship
  • DConnections with entrepreneurs, investors, and business leaders

  • Initiative

    Recognize a problem worth solving.

  • Judgment

    Test assumptions and understand the market.

  • Execution

    Turn an idea into practical next steps.

The skills are already there.
The terrain is new.

02 / The journey

How an idea takes shape

Build your understanding, develop an idea with a team, and learn how to present a venture. The program moves through three stages.

Stage 01 / 03 The Foundations

  1. Stage 01

    The Foundations

    Understand the fundamentals of entrepreneurship and identify an opportunity worth exploring.

    What you’ll cover
    • Entrepreneurial thinking and opportunity discovery
    • Ideation with AI tools
    • Lean Startup
    • Business Model Canvas and MVP
    • Customer understanding, market size, and competition
    • Team building
    • AI tools for entrepreneurs
    • Presentation skills
    • Initial team and idea presentations

  2. Stage 02

    Build and Test

    Work with your team to turn an initial idea into something you can test.

    This is the independent work period between the two teaching blocks. Teams form, choose an idea, and test it with real customers.

    What you’ll cover
    • Team formation and idea selection
    • Business model development
    • Market, competitor, and opportunity mapping
    • MVP development
    • Testing with customers
    • Preparation for venture presentations

  3. Stage 03

    Pre-Acceleration

    Develop your understanding of product, growth, business, and investment, then prepare to present your venture.

    What you’ll cover
    • Digital marketing strategy for startups
    • UX and product design
    • Sales and business models
    • Legal aspects of entrepreneurship
    • Investor practice and speed dating
    • Fundraising from angels and venture capital funds
    • Product management
    • Pitch rehearsal
    • Final venture presentations
